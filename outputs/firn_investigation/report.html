Firn power plateau investigation — MCoRDS-matched chirp

Exploratory experiment, not a gate. Records the Culberg & Schroeder (2020) plateau diagnostics on point-sampled B26 firn stacks, now with the MCoRDS 2017 P-3 waveform (chirp B = 30 MHz, hann compression window, Tpd = 3 μs; provenance outputs/cache/mcords_2017P3_params.json). The paper's plateau definition is operational: near-zero/nonnegative smoothed-trace gradient over depth intervals > 10 m, a near-surface secondary maximum typically 10–15 dB below the surface return, sharp decay below ~60 m. The previous DELTA-pulse sweep is archived in delta_runs/.

Waveform / grid. dt = 4 ns, n = 640 (same 2560 ns twtt window as the delta sweep's 512 × 5 ns). Alias rule: |f0 − round(f0·dt)/dt| = 55 MHz > B/2 = 15 MHz — the in-band-alias warning is asserted silent on every run. Chirped range resolution (hann, 1.44·c/2B): 7.2 m air / 4.4 m in-firn — N ≥ 40 stacks (mean spacing ≤ 3 m) are FULLY UNRESOLVED; N = 10/20 (12 / 6 m) remain resolved.

Depth-power profiles

Method. Coherent fields summed over layers, |.|^2, trace-averaged; twtt mapped to depth through the per-layer in-firn nadir times; linear power boxcar-smoothed over 5 m (≈5.5 m effective); dB relative to the raw surface peak. Dashed curves are the surface-field-subtracted profiles |E_total − E_surface|² (same smoothing and normalization): the kernel is single-bounce linear, so subtracting the surface's own complex field exactly isolates the layer signal from the surface off-nadir response that contaminates the upper ~45 m. E_surface is the run's own layer-0 field; its ratio-scaled agreement with the surface-only reference field is recorded per run (surface_field_check_rel). eps is POINT-sampled from the 0.1 m-smoothed B26 core (Kovacs eps(rho)).

Delta-pulse sweep vs MCoRDS-matched chirp

The archived delta sweep (in delta_runs/) used dt = 5 ns, which places the envelope-quantization alias at |f_a| = 5 MHz -- IN the 30 MHz band; the chirped sweep uses dt = 4 ns (|f_a| = 55 MHz > B/2 = 15 MHz), so the compressed pulse rejects the quantization noise entirely.

Findings

Is the plateau there now? Yes -- the paper's operational criterion is met. With the MCoRDS-matched chirp all 16 layered runs plus the reference completed (no cutoff skips; the previously-skipped random N=80 seeds ran in ~31 s each on the batched compiled kernel). The longest contiguous interval with smoothed-trace gradient ≥ -0.05 dB/m exceeds the paper's 10 m in 6/16 runs on the RAW profile (up to 18.0 m, random N=80 s1 at 42-60 m depth; also 13.3 m at 30-43 m for s2 and 10.3-11.4 m elsewhere) and in 11/16 runs on the SURFACE-SUBTRACTED profile (10.3-18.7 m; every random N=20 and N=40 run, three of four N=80 runs). In the delta sweep NO run met the criterion (best 9.6 m). The subtracted intervals at N=20-80 often start essentially at the surface (e.g. [0.1, 16-18 m]) -- the paper's near-surface plateau -- while the N=80 raw intervals sit mid-firn (30-60 m). Threshold sensitivity (0 / -0.1 dB/m) changes the long intervals by < 1 m. (Caution: equal-placement raw intervals at 99-117 m ride the flattened deep tail of a periodic stack; random placement is the physical case.)

Which change mattered? All three, in different depth bands. (1) Alias-free chirp (dt 5 -> 4 ns, |f_a| 5 -> 55 MHz): the surface-only reference floor fell 6-17 dB over 5-45 m apparent depth (5-10 m: -16.8 -> -23.3 dB; 10-20 m: -17.3 -> -34.3; 20-45 m: -24.7 -> -36.5). Most of the delta sweep's "upper ~45 m surface contamination" was the in-band envelope-quantization alias, not physical off-nadir response: with it gone the layered profiles sit 10-20 dB above the reference beyond ~10 m, so raw and subtracted profiles agree there. (2) Pulse integration: the 4.4 m in-firn hann pulse integrates several interfaces per resolution cell; deep bands rise 2-6 dB over the delta profiles (equal N=80, 100-119 m: -40.4 -> -34.1 dB), flattening the apparent decay -- part of why gradients now clear the threshold. (3) Surface-field subtraction: decisive only in the top ~15 m, where the surface main lobe lives: the raw secondary maximum is pinned at ~5 m depth (-4.0..-8.8 dB, the surface-lobe shoulder) in every run, while the subtracted profiles remove it exactly (random N=20 s0, 5-10 m band: -20.2 -> -59.0 dB where that stack has no shallow layers) and expose the near-surface plateaus. (4) N=80 random (newly completed): supplies the two longest RAW plateaus -- the criterion is met without subtraction only at N=80.

Secondary maximum. On the subtracted profiles it lands at -10.2..-23.0 dB relative to the surface peak (median ~ -17.5 dB) at 5-65 m depth -- the paper's "typically 10-15 dB below the surface" regime, slightly weak on average. One outlier: random N=10 s2 has real layers at 3.98/4.34 m whose merged echo reaches -1.4 dB. The raw secondary maxima are all the ~5 m surface shoulder and are not layer diagnostics.

Resolved-to-unresolved transition. Mean layer spacing 12.0/6.0/3.0/ 1.5 m (N=10/20/40/80) against the 4.4 m in-firn chirped resolution: N=10/20 are resolved (echo trains, visible in the panels), N≥40 fully unresolved (continuous profiles). The criterion tracks it: subtracted pass rate 1/4 at N=10 vs 10/12 at N≥20; raw passes concentrate at N=80. The deep floor is still rising at N=80 (50-100 m random-mean: about -31 dB at N=10-40 -> -27 dB at N=80; 100-119 m: -46 (N=20) -> -36 dB) -- band levels move 3-6 dB per doubling of N, i.e. NOT converged at N=80. Realized per-interface |gamma| medians (-40.5 to -52.9 dB, N=10 -> 80) remain ~30-40 dB above the full-resolution 1 mm adjacent-sample statistics: an 80-layer point-sampled stack is still far from a converged discretisation of the continuous core.

Joint-solve (D+) trigger verdict. The trigger's first condition -- "the firn plateau investigation shows the phenomenon emerging with layer count" -- is met: plateau length and pass rate grow with N, the raw-profile criterion is first met at N=80, and nothing has converged by N=80 while the sequential-chain compile wall (O(N^2): 26.6 min at N=80) blocks the N ~ 150-300 runs a convergence study needs. The nuance: the operational plateau no longer NEEDS larger N -- chirp + subtraction demonstrate it at feasible layer counts -- so D+ should be scheduled when the CONVERGED absolute levels and fine-layer statistics (comparison against a 1-D transfer-matrix referee, C&S-style) matter for instrument work, not for plateau morphology alone.

Rigor notes. Every simulate() call ran with the in-band-alias warning asserted silent (|f_a| = 55 MHz > B/2 = 15 MHz at dt = 4 ns). The surface-subtraction identity was cross-checked per run: the run's layer-0 field equals the surface-only reference field scaled by the surface-gamma ratio to ≤ 1.4e-6 of the reference peak (exactly 0 for equal placements, whose top-medium eps matches the reference).
